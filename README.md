# Rural-vs-Urban-Consumer-Price-Index-Analysis

### India Consumer Price Index (CPI) Analysis | Excel

**Project Duration:** 2013–2023 dataset analysis

**Tools & Technologies:** Microsoft Excel, CSV, Data Analysis

**Project Description:**

Analyzed India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data from January 2013 to May 2023 to understand inflation trends across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.

**Key Responsibilities & Achievements:**
- Cleaned and prepared CPI datasets by identifying missing observations, inconsistent month formats, and data quality issues.
- Used Excel functions such as COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIF, and DATEDIF to perform data calculations and analysis.
- Analyzed average General CPI values across Rural (140.8), Urban (138.2), and Rural+Urban (139.6) sectors.
- Compared CPI trends across expenditure categories, identifying significant index increases in Spices, Meat and Fish, Health, and Clothing.
- Evaluated long-term CPI changes and identified differences in rural and urban price index levels.
- Derived business recommendations related to regional pricing strategies, procurement planning, and consumer affordability.
